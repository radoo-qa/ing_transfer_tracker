# Privacy Policy — ING Transfer Racker

Effective date: October 8, 2026

## 1. About the application

ING Transfer Racker is a personal, non-commercial Node.js application intended to run locally on the owner's computer.

The application is designed exclusively for accessing and managing the owner's own financial information. It is not offered as a service to other users.

## 2. Information processed

When authorized by the account owner, the application may access:

- Bank account identifiers and account details.
- Account balances.
- Transaction history, including dates, amounts and descriptions.

This information is used exclusively for personal financial management.

## 3. Banking authorization

Bank account access is provided through Enable Banking API.

Authentication and consent are handled through the participating financial institution and Enable Banking.

The application is not intended to collect or store online banking passwords.

## 4. Local data processing

The application operates on the owner's local computer.

Depending on its implementation, retrieved financial information may be processed temporarily or stored locally.

The application is not offered as a hosted cloud service.

## 5. External services

Enable Banking and participating financial institutions may process information necessary to establish authorized account connections.

Their respective privacy policies also apply.

## 6. Data retention and security

The application owner is responsible for protecting locally stored information, API credentials and authorization tokens.

Locally stored financial information can be deleted by the owner when no longer needed.

## 7. Changes

This policy will be updated if the application's functionality, data storage or processing practices change.
